## Full description (max 4000 chars)

College Executive Function (CEF) turns your syllabi, course documents, and calendars into a single, synchronized academic plan — so you spend less time managing your semester and more time studying.

HOW IT WORKS
• Import a syllabus, course PDF, Google Doc, or .ics calendar file — including from Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive, or any cloud storage app enabled under Files.
• CEF reads it and extracts every deadline, exam, class session, and administrative date automatically.
• Your schedule syncs to Google Calendar, so it lives alongside everything else you already track.

BUILT FOR EXECUTIVE FUNCTION
• Break It Down (AI) — turn a big deliverable (final paper, project, exam) into a sequence of dated study steps, so "write the essay" becomes a plan you can actually follow.
• Study progress tracking on every deadline, so you always know how far ahead — or behind — you are.
• Weekly routine support and a study/scheduling window the AI respects when it plans your time.
• Multi-source chat — ask questions across everything you've uploaded at once.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• CEF uses your own free AI key from Google (Gemini) — entered once in Settings — so the app stays free and your documents are processed under your own account.
• Your calendar event contents, syllabus files, and documents are never sold or shared.
• Works on Android, iOS, and desktop.
